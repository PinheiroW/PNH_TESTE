# Humans 1.4.4

Human fists now apply 20 health damage and 20 shock to an unprotected player's torso. From 100 health and 100 shock, four rapid unblocked punches leave 20 health and 20 shock, below DayZ's 25-shock unconscious threshold. Normal armor, blocking and shock recovery remain active, so protected players or punches spaced far apart can take more hits to cause knockout. Existing injuries or low shock can cause earlier unconsciousness. Another unprotected punch can kill the unconscious player.

Weapons retain their vanilla damage and shock. Civilian weapon spawn chance remains JSON-configurable at 75% by default; soldiers retain bats.

Built and signed; resource validation passed. Damage values and the native unconscious threshold were checked against installed DayZ sources. A live four-punch knockout test has not been performed for this release.
